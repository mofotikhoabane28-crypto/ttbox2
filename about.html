About & help

What G-box is, how it works and who to ask.

The idea

G-box is a small streaming platform built around four shelves: films, series, documentaries and short educational courses. It was designed in black and blue so posters stand out and the interface stays quiet.

Everything here is original sample content made for a web design assignment: posters, trailers, titles and descriptions.

How to use it

  1. Browse or search the catalogue, then filter by type or genre.
  2. Open a title for details and a trailer.
  3. Press + My List to save it. Your list stays after a refresh.
